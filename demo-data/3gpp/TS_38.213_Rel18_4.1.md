# TS 38.213 — NR; Physical layer procedures for control

**3GPP Release:** Rel-18
**Version:** 18.3.0
**Date:** 2024-06

## 4 UE Procedure for Reporting Channel State Information

### 4.1 Physical Layer Measurements

#### 4.1.1 SS-RSRP (SS Reference Signal Received Power)

SS-RSRP is defined as the linear average over the power contributions of the resource elements that carry secondary synchronization signals (SSS).

The reference point for the SS-RSRP shall be the antenna connector of the UE.

**Measurement quantity mapping to RRC (TS 38.331 clause 5.5.4):**
- SS-RSRP maps to `rsrp` field in MeasQuantityResults
- Reporting range: -156 to -31 dBm (RSRP-Range 0..127)

#### 4.1.2 SS-RSRQ (SS Reference Signal Received Quality)

SS-RSRQ is defined as N × SS-RSRP / (NR carrier RSSI), where N is the number of resource blocks in the NR carrier RSSI measurement bandwidth.

**Measurement quantity mapping to RRC (TS 38.331 clause 5.5.4):**
- SS-RSRQ maps to `rsrq` field in MeasQuantityResults
- Reporting range: -43 to 20 dB (RSRQ-Range 0..127)

#### 4.1.3 SS-SINR (SS Signal-to-Interference-plus-Noise Ratio)

SS-SINR is defined as the linear average over the power contribution of the resource elements carrying SSS divided by the linear average of the noise and interference power contribution.

**Measurement quantity mapping to RRC (TS 38.331 clause 5.5.4):**
- SS-SINR maps to `sinr` field in MeasQuantityResults
- Reporting range: -23 to 40 dB (SINR-Range 0..127)

#### 4.1.4 CSI-RS Based Measurements

For CSI-RS based measurements, the UE measures:
- CSI-RSRP: analogous to SS-RSRP but on CSI-RS resources
- CSI-RSRQ: analogous to SS-RSRQ but on CSI-RS resources
- CSI-SINR: analogous to SS-SINR but on CSI-RS resources

These map to the `resultsCSI-RS-Cell` field in MeasResultNR (TS 38.331).

```asn1
-- ASN1START
-- Imported from TS 38.331
IMPORTS
    RSRP-Range,
    RSRQ-Range,
    SINR-Range
FROM NR-RRC-Definitions;

PhysMeasQuantity ::= ENUMERATED {
    ss-rsrp,
    ss-rsrq,
    ss-sinr,
    csi-rsrp,
    csi-rsrq,
    csi-sinr
}
-- ASN1STOP
```

### 4.2 L1-RSRP Reporting

L1-RSRP is used for beam management procedures. Unlike L3 measurements reported via MeasurementReport (TS 38.331 clause 5.5.4), L1-RSRP is reported via UCI on PUCCH or PUSCH.

The UE reports L1-RSRP for up to 4 beams as configured by higher layers.

### 4.3 Measurement Filtering

The physical layer applies Layer 1 filtering before reporting to higher layers. The filter coefficient is configured via TS 38.331 quantityConfig IE.

Layer 3 filtering (applied by RRC layer) uses the formula:
```
F_n = (1 - a) × F_{n-1} + a × M_n
```
where a = 1/2^(k/4) and k is the filterCoefficient from TS 38.331.
